How to win

Build or buy. Launch your own brand into a market and grow it — new brands turn advertising into recognition far faster for their first minute — or pay a premium to take someone else's and get their whole slice today.

Price is a trade. Charge more per unit and lose customers — badly in Airlines and Grocery, hardly at all in Semiconductors.

Campaigns are one tap. A blitz buys recognition, a promotion discounts you for a while, a push grows the whole market's demand.

You can only raid the weak. Take a rival's brand when it's starved below 15% of its market, when its parent is deep in debt, or when you out-hold that parent there. Firms with three brands or fewer are off-limits.

Scale pays. Brands you run side by side in one market share their costs.
