How it works

  1. Districts pay rent. Each one you hold adds income every second; neighbours you also own add a synergy bonus.
  2. Expansion is spending. Click any district on your border to pour capital into it. Neutral markets are cheap; a rival's stake costs more to buy out.
  3. Holdings entrench. Districts you keep harden over time, so an unanswered lead is hard to reverse.
  4. Reinvestment sets how much of your treasury goes into expansion each second. Everything else compounds.
  5. Win at 60% of total demand — a monopoly — or by outlasting every rival.
