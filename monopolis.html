How to win

Share is bought two ways. Cut your price or out-advertise a rival to take customers inside a market; buy the brand outright to take the whole slice at once.

Price is a trade. High prices earn more per unit and bleed share — badly in price-sensitive categories like Airlines and Grocery, barely at all in Semiconductors.

Marketing decays. Equity is what you're really buying, and it rots the second you stop paying for it.

You can only raid the weak. A brand is takeable when it's starved below 15% of its market, its parent is over-leveraged, or you already out-hold that parent in that category. So win a category first, then buy the losers in it.

Costs rise all game. Standoffs get expensive; someone's balance sheet always breaks. Reach 50% of all revenue before it's yours.
