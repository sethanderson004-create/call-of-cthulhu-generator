Tap a segment on your border to push into it.

Tap once to push into a market, again to hold it, again to stop. A dashed line shows money leaving your hub for a market, labelled with what it costs per second — yours and everyone else's. Once you hold more than half of a market you set the price there and it pays more than its size, so finishing a market competes with starting another.

Past about a quarter of the economy you start losing income to scrutiny, and it bites harder the closer anyone comes to owning half — so the last stretch is the hardest one.

Segments are markets, sized by how big they are. Colour fills outward from the hub as a firm takes share; a full spoke is a cornered market. You can only push into a segment touching one you hold. Brightness on a border is capital massing there — it shows a few seconds before share starts moving, so a push is visible before it lands. A bright hub means someone has stopped spending and is banking.
