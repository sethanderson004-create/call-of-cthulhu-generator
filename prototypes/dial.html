Tap a segment on your border to push into it.

Segments are markets, sized by how big they are. Colour fills outward from the hub as a firm takes share; a full spoke is a cornered market. You can only push into a segment touching one you hold. Brightness on a border is capital massing there — it shows a few seconds before share starts moving, so a push is visible before it lands. A bright hub means someone has stopped spending and is banking.
